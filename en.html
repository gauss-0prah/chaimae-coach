Frequently asked questions

Got questions? That's normal.

What's the difference between coaching and therapy?

Coaching focuses on the present and on action: clarifying your goals, moving past your blocks and building new habits. It does not replace medical or psychological care. If your situation calls for it, I'll refer you to a health professional.

How do the sessions work?

Sessions take place by video on Zoom, once a week for the program, and you can join from anywhere in the world. Between sessions you have exercises to practise, and I'm available on WhatsApp.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. The Elevate & Thrive program (3,600 MAD) can be paid in full or in 3 instalments of 1,200 MAD.

Does the discovery session commit me to anything?

No, there's no commitment. It's a chance to get to know each other, understand what you're going through and see whether my coaching is right for you.

Is what I share kept confidential?

Absolutely. Everything said in a session stays strictly between us. My role is to give you a safe, caring and judgement-free space.

What language are the sessions in?

Sessions are mainly held in French. If you'd prefer English or another language, message me on WhatsApp and we'll work it out together.

What if this is the moment to take care of yourself?
